Help & Tips

Please disable silent mode on your mobile device for audio playback.

Press the spacebar to play or pause the loop.

Use number keys 1–9 to mute or unmute the corresponding instruments.

If the number circles are red, it means either the stem has not been generated yet or the channel is muted.

A red border around a card indicates that the instrument or channel is muted.

Generations are not 100% predictable due to the nature of AI – experiment and generate multiple versions to find your favorite take.
